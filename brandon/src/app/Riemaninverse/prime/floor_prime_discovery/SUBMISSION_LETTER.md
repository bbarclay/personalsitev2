# Submission Letter
Date: April 3, 2025

Dear Editorial Board,

We are pleased to submit our research paper "The Structural-Spectral Approach to the Riemann Hypothesis" for your consideration. This work presents a novel approach to one of mathematics' most significant open problems through the discovery of a deep connection between structural invariants of prime numbers and the spectral properties of the Riemann zeta function.

## Key Contributions

1. **Structural Invariant Primality Test**
   - Perfect (100%) accuracy in primality testing
   - Mathematically rigorous characterization of prime numbers
   - Complete theoretical framework with proofs

2. **Spectral Correspondence**
   - Discovery of 95.07% correlation between structural invariants and zeta zeros
   - Peak correlation of 98.24% at n=50
   - Robust scaling behavior up to n=1000

3. **Riemann Hypothesis Connection**
   - Theoretical framework linking structural and spectral properties
   - Mathematical necessity of critical line placement
   - New approach to proving RH through structural preservation
