## Connections to Class Field Theory

Class field theory provides a comprehensive framework for understanding abelian extensions of number fields. Our approach relies on properties of cyclotomic fields, which play a central role in class field theory.

### 1. Artin Reciprocity

Artin reciprocity establishes a correspondence between abelian extensions and moduli with associated ray class groups.

**Connection Insight:** Our structural invariant examines properties of Gal(Q(ζₙ)/Q), which is exactly the kind of Galois group that Artin reciprocity characterizes. The fact that primality is encoded in this structure suggests deep connections to the fundamental mechanisms of class field theory.

**Exploration Direction:** Investigate whether the structural invariant can be reinterpreted in terms of Artin reciprocity, potentially revealing connections to more general class field theoretic constructs.
